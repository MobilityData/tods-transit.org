# TODS Board Composition Policy

In standing up and sustaining the TODS Board, the current [TODS Board][board]:

- MUST decide the number of board positions;
	- MUST NOT eliminate board positions unless they are vacant;
	- MUST have 85% or more of the voting membership approve any changes to the board composition;
- MUST select individuals to fill open voting board positions for three-year terms;
- MUST stagger board terms into three or more cohorts to avoid more than half the board turning over in any given year;
- SHOULD aim for geographic, demographic, and organizational diversity; and
- SHOULD aim for the majority of board members to come from transit providers.

Individuals serving on the [TODS Board][board]:

- MUST serve as individuals, not organization representatives;
- SHOULD offer their resignation with significant professional changes, like switching industries;
- MAY be removed by a two-thirds vote of the voting [TODS Board][board] members for infractions including but not limited to not upholding the TODS Code of Conduct or their [TODS Board][board] responsibilities;
	- MUST be notified via email and the MobilityData Slack channel at least 21 calendar days in advance of the regularly-scheduled meeting at which their removal is to be considered;
	- SHALL be permitted to speak at the meeting prior to the board taking such a vote for their removal;
- MUST NOT serve more than two consecutive terms, or portions thereof, without spending at least 10 consecutive months without a voting seat on the board; and
	- MAY, during this time, serve as non-voting "emeritus members", with all of the rights, privileges, and honors of membership besides voting eligibility;
	- Emeritus members MUST NOT be counted as members for the sake of any vote, but SHALL have the right to speak prior to any vote in the same manner as other board members;
	- Notwithstanding the foregoing, Term 0 below SHALL NOT be considered in determining applicable term restrictions.
